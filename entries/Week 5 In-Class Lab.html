In-Class Lab

October 7, 2026 · Week 5

Spent some time this time on the earlier part of design research, actually looking at the ssscenario and coming up with our own questions to interrogate the problem at hand and come up with a solution. Of course we didn't have any people to ask about these fictional scenarios so we had AI generate responses to questions we came up with, and noting it down. We split into groups with each of us coming up with questions for one person in each scenario, i.e, for the case of a coffee shop looking to open hours longer, I came up with questions to interview the owner. Quite a good practice if a bit rough by using AI for it but possibly pretty good warmup for the final project.
